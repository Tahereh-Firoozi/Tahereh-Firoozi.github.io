01About me

“How can assessment be more informative and scalable, while protecting validity and fairness?”

The question at the centre of my research program

Welcome! I am a Clinical Assistant Professor in the Mike Petryk School of Dentistry, Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry, and an Adjunct Assistant Professor in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science (MEDS) in the Faculty of Education at the University of Alberta.

My research career spans more than a decade in assessment and measurement. Since my early work on test validity and differential item functioning, I have developed a research program in multilingual automated scoring, automatic item generation, and efficient use of annotated data — connecting rigorous assessment research with practical questions about clinical learning and performance.

I hold two doctorates, in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science (University of Alberta) and Applied Linguistics (Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz), and completed an Alberta Innovates Postdoctoral Fellowship. My collaborations extend across education, medicine, dentistry, and computing science.
