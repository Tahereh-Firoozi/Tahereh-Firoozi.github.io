# University of Alberta visual identity implementation

Reference: user-supplied 2025/26 Master Brand Guidelines, reviewed in full.
- pp. 8–13: official primary logo; unchanged proportions and colour, separate placement; shield minimum 20px and clear space at least one shield width.
- pp. 16–20: University of Alberta name; Roboto family. One font throughout preserves the user’s preference.
- pp. 22–26: primary green #275D38, gold #F2CD00, digital white #F5F5F5 and digital black #2B292A; sparse tints; contrast and whitespace.
- pp. 34–39: illustration flexibility within the palette; do not invent or modify the official UA graphic.
- pp. 40–55: university mark remains standalone; no personal-name/department lockup.
