02 — BiosketchDr. Tahereh Firoozi

Biosketch

A research career spanning more than a decade in assessment and measurement — from test validity and differential item functioning to AI-supported assessment in education and the health professions.

01

Profile

Dr. Tahereh Firoozi connects rigorous assessment research with practical questions about clinical learning and performance.

She is a Clinical Assistant Professor in the Mike Petryk School of Dentistry and an Adjunct Assistant Professor in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science at the University of Alberta. She holds a Ph.D. in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science from the University of Alberta (2023) and a Ph.D. in Applied Linguistics (Measurement and Testing) from Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz (2019), and completed an Alberta Innovates Postdoctoral Fellowship in the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry (2024–2025). She previously served as a Senior Psychometrician with Alberta Education.

Since her early work on test validity and differential item functioning, she has developed a research program in multilingual automated scoring, automatic item generation, and efficient use of annotated data. Her dissertation developed and evaluated multilingual BERT for scoring written essays in Persian, and informed an invited chapter in the Routledge International Handbook of Automated Essay Evaluation.

Her health professions research examines competency-based medical education, natural language processing of narrative feedback and clinical text, and retrieval-augmented systems for clinical reasoning. Her work appears in the Journal of Educational Measurement, Educational Measurement: Issues and Practice, PLOS Digital Health, and the Journal of Graduate Medical Education, and her collaborations extend across education, medicine, dentistry, and computing science.
