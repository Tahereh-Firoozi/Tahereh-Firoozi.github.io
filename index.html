    I am a Clinical Assistant Professor at the Mike Petryk School of Dentistry and an Adjunct Assistant Professor in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science at the University of Alberta.

    My research integrates language, data science, and educational measurement to develop and evaluate natural language processing (NLP) applications in dentistry, medicine, and education. My work focuses on transformer-based language models, knowledge graphs, graph-based retrieval-augmented generation, multilingual automated essay scoring, and automatic item generation.

    My NLP expertise is grounded in two complementary PhDs—in Applied Linguistics and in Measurement, Evaluation, and Data Science—combining knowledge of language with computational methods. I further developed this expertise through an Alberta Innovates Postdoctoral Fellowship in dentistry at the University of Alberta.
