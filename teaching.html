05 — TeachingDr. Tahereh Firoozi

Teaching

Graduate and undergraduate teaching in research methods, assessment, statistics, and natural language processing — in education, dentistry, and medicine.

01

Graduate courses · University of Alberta

EDPY 501Fall 2026
Research Methodology in EducationPrincipal InstructorThis introductory graduate course covers foundational educational and psychological research concepts, principles, and techniques, including quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods.
DENT 543Winter 2026
Introduction to Dental Analytics and Data ManagementPrincipal InstructorThis graduate-level course introduces key principles and applications in dental analytics and data management, with an emphasis on machine learning and clinical chart mining. Students examine how natural language processing and machine-learning approaches can be applied to structured and unstructured dental and healthcare data, including narrative notes and patient records, to extract meaningful information, identify patterns, and support clinical decision-making and research.
EDHS 507Spring 2025
Assessment in Health SciencesPrincipal InstructorThis graduate-level course focuses on key concepts and principles of educational assessment, equipping students with the foundational knowledge required to evaluate and make informed inferences about students’ knowledge, skills, and competencies.
DENT 545Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026
Special Topics in Oral Health ResearchPrincipal InstructorI delivered this course for the first time at the School of Dentistry, where I independently developed the syllabus and all instructional materials specifically for a dental education context. The purpose of this graduate-level course is to introduce natural language processing (NLP) techniques in health research and equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to implement computational techniques for extracting, analyzing, and interpreting information from textual healthcare data.
EDPY 500Winter 2023
Introduction to Data Analysis in Educational ResearchPrincipal InstructorThis graduate-level course introduces univariate statistical data-analysis techniques in educational research, the application of quantitative research methods, and the analysis of educational data using SPSS.
MED 606Summer 2024, Summer 2025, Fall 2026
Translational Research Training Program: Introduction to Statistics for Clinical ResearchLecturerThis intensive course introduces research methodology, with particular emphasis on multivariate statistical analysis, to medical trainees in the infectious disease residency program.
EDPY-501-Fall-2026Course site for EDPY 501DENT-545-Winter-2026Introduction to Machine Learning for Students at Dental School

03

Workshops

July 23–26, 2024 · University of AlbertaNCOHR Summer Institute, Artificial Intelligence in DentistryThe workshop was funded by the Network for Canadian Oral Health Research (NCOHR) and hosted by the School of Dentistry. It included 20 faculty members and graduate students from across Canada and focused on AI methods for text and image mining in dentistry. As one of the workshop chairs and the principal instructor for the first two days, I taught the theory and application of natural language processing (NLP), including transformer-based models in dentistry.
March 2018 · Queen’s UniversityIntroduction to Factor AnalysisI instructed a one-day workshop introducing factor-analysis methods and software to graduate students in the Faculty of Education at Queen’s University.
April 2015 · Shahid Chamran University of AhvazIntroduction to Item Response TheoryI instructed a one-day workshop introducing the Rasch model using Winsteps to graduate students in TEFL at Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz.
