Graduate courses

DENT 545Graduate

Special Topics in Oral Health Research

University of AlbertaFall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026Principal Instructor

I delivered this course for the first time at the School of Dentistry, where I independently developed the syllabus and all instructional materials specifically for a dental education context. The purpose of this graduate-level course is to introduce natural language processing (NLP) techniques in health research and equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to implement computational techniques for extracting, analysing, and interpreting information from textual healthcare data.

DENT 543Graduate

Introduction to Dental Analytics and Data Management

University of AlbertaWinter 2026Principal Instructor

This graduate-level course introduces key principles and applications in dental analytics and data management, with an emphasis on machine learning and clinical chart mining. Students examine how natural language processing and machine-learning approaches can be applied to structured and unstructured dental and healthcare data, including narrative notes and patient records, to extract meaningful information, identify patterns, and support clinical decision-making and research.

EDPY 501Graduate

EDHS 507Graduate

Assessment in Health Sciences

EDPY 500Graduate

Introduction to Data Analysis in Educational Research

NCOHR Summer Institute: Artificial Intelligence in Dentistry

University of Alberta

The workshop was funded by the Network for Canadian Oral Health Research (NCOHR) and hosted by the School of Dentistry.
